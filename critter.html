Critter

Rank what you watch. Argue about it with people you trust. No stars out of five: you compare each film or show against what you've already seen, and Critter puts it exactly where it belongs.

How it works

Your #1 is your #1. Everything else sits somewhere under it.

Head to head

Pick between two titles you've seen. A few picks place a new film or show exactly where it belongs, with a score to match.

Takes

One line per title, no plot summary. Your feed lights up when something becomes your new No. 1 or cracks your top 10.

Your people

Follow friends, see what they're ranking, and find out whose taste actually holds up.

Lists & watchlist

Make lists to share or keep private, and keep track of everything you haven't got to yet.

Take a look

The feed, your profile, search, and your ranked list.

Support

Questions, bugs, or a report about content or another user: email support@wolverineinteractive.com. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything you've posted.

Reporting and blocking

To report a take or reply, press and hold it and choose Report. To report or block a person, tap ••• on their profile.

Critter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Critter is a service for ranking films and television and sharing those rankings with people you choose to follow. This policy describes what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Critter is operated by Wolverine Interactive LLC. Contact: support@wolverineinteractive.com.

What we collect

Your account. An email address and a password. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store the password itself.

What you create. Your rankings and their scores, the takes you write, lists you make, replies you post, who you follow, your display name, your handle, your bio, and a profile picture if you upload one.

Notifications. If you allow push notifications, your device gives us a push token so we can deliver them. It identifies your device to Apple's notification service, not to anyone else. Turn notifications off in iOS Settings and we stop using it.

Technical data. Our hosting provider records ordinary server logs, including IP address and timestamps, for security and debugging.

We do not collect your contacts, your location, your advertising identifier, or data from other apps. Critter contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Why we collect it

Your email identifies your account and lets you reset your password. Everything you create is stored so the app can show it back to you, and to the people you share it with. Server logs exist to keep the service working and secure.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What other people can see

Your display name, handle, bio, profile picture, rankings, scores, takes and public lists are visible to other Critter users. Lists marked private are visible only to you. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Where it is stored

Account data and everything you create are stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, on servers in the United States. Film and television information comes from The Movie Database (TMDB); we send TMDB your search terms in order to return results, and nothing identifying you.

Keeping it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your rankings, takes, lists, follows, profile and login are removed. It cannot be undone, and we cannot recover it afterwards.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, and export or correct your data by writing to support@wolverineinteractive.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR or CCPA — including access, correction, deletion and portability. Write to us and we will honour them.

Children

Critter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note the change in the app.

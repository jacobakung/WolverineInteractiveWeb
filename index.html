About the studio

Wolverine Interactive LLC is a small independent studio. We design, build, and ship our own games, and we stay close to the people who play them.

We like games with a simple hook and real depth: easy to pick up in the first ten seconds, hard to put down after that.

  • Quick to learn

    No tutorials you have to sit through. The first round teaches you the game.

  • Built for short sessions

    Rounds that fit a bus ride, with reasons to come back tomorrow.

  • Made by people who play them

    Every mode ships because we wanted to play it ourselves.

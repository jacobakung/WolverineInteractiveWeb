WordRush

Drag across adjacent letter tiles to build words before the timer runs out. Easy to learn, tough to master, and built for one-more-round sessions.

Download on the App Store
  • iOS

Four ways to play

Every mode uses the same board and the same rule: connect touching tiles, longer words score more.

Classic

A timed round on a 4×4 or 5×5 board. Find as many words as you can before the clock hits zero.

Daily Board

One shared board every day. Everyone gets the same letters, so the only variable is you.

Marathon

Keep the run alive. Words buy you time, and the board keeps refilling for as long as you can keep up.

Quick Match

Real-time 1v1 against another player on the same board. Win to climb the trophy ladder.

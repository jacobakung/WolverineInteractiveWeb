RateMyJumper

Film your jump shot from the side and find out how it really looks. The crowd rates it head-to-head, and an AI baseline compares your form to the NBA's best shooters.

Download on the App Store
  • iOS

How it works

Your shot is shown as a silhouette, so the rating is about the form, not the face.

Upload your jumper

Record a side-angle clip. The app checks it on your phone before anything is sent, then turns it into a clean silhouette.

Crowd rating

Players pick the better shot in head-to-head matchups. Those picks become your 1 to 10 rating.

AI baseline

Elbow angle, release angle, knee bend, base width, and tempo are measured and compared to top NBA shooting forms.

Guess the NBA Jumper

Name the pro from their silhouette. A new Daily Showdown every day, plus an endless mode with streaks and leaderboards.
